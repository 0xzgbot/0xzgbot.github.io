ShopPilot

A Mac app that designs the cut, shows you the cut, then runs the mill.

Draw the shape, the app figures out the toolpaths, you watch a preview of material getting carved away, then you send it to the machine. It will not run just because a file opened. Forty-seven checks look at the G-code before anything moves.

03

Cinesmith

A movie crew that lives on computers in the room. Brief in, frames out.

You write the brief. A helper plans the shots, renders them, checks them, and remembers what worked. Characters stay consistent without sending faces to the cloud. It keeps a log of what fired, what failed, and what finally passed. Hardware in the shop: a DGX Spark plus two 3090s.

Hermes ComfyUI Skills

Five commands that load a full picture-and-video playbook on your own hardware.

Type one slash command and the helper loads a whole set of skills at once: prompting, stills, video, local shop, overnight batches. After every render, a vision check has to look at the frame and approve it. The generator saying “done” is not a pass. It runs on machines in the room first. Cloud is only backup. It can keep rendering overnight on a schedule.
